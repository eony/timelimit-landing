How tracking works

Time that reflects real attention

Many trackers count every second a tab is open. Time & Limit counts the time you actually spend, using a few clear rules:

  • The tab in front of you counts. Switching tabs or apps moves the clock with you.
  • Away means away. After 2 minutes without keyboard or mouse input, tracking pauses and those 2 idle minutes are taken back off.
  • Watching still counts. A video playing in the tab you're on keeps counting, even with your hands off the keyboard.
  • Background audio is your call. Music in a hidden tab only counts if you turn on “Track Background Audio”.
  • Locking your screen stops everything. So does putting your computer to sleep.

Limits & schedules

Boundaries that hold, without nagging

Give a site 30 minutes a day and the toolbar badge counts down as you use it. You'll get a notification at 5 minutes and again at 1 minute left, and when time is up the site is blocked until midnight. The block happens the moment the limit runs out, not a few seconds later.

Want guaranteed distraction-free hours? Set a schedule and every limited site is blocked automatically during the hours and days you choose, such as weekdays 9 to 5.

Privacy

Your browsing stays on your computer

A tool that sees every site you visit should earn your trust. Time & Limit stores everything in your browser's own storage. There are no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and the extension's pages make no requests to third parties: even fonts and site icons are loaded locally.

Settings sync is optional and off by default. When you turn it on, only your rules and preferences are shared through Chrome Sync; your browsing history never is. You can export or delete your data whenever you like.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Time & Limit really free?

Yes. There are no premium tiers, subscriptions, ads or hidden fees.

Does it send my browsing data anywhere?

No. All tracking happens and is stored inside your browser. The only optional exception is settings sync, which shares your rules (never your history) through your own Chrome account.

How does a daily limit work?

Set minutes per day for a site, such as 30 minutes for youtube.com. Time on the site and its subdomains adds up towards it. You're warned at 5 minutes and 1 minute left, and when the time is used up the site is blocked until midnight, when every limit resets.

What counts as time, and what doesn't?

The tab you're looking at counts. If you're away for more than 2 minutes, tracking pauses and the idle minutes are removed, unless a video or audio is playing in that tab. Music in hidden tabs counts only if you turn on “Track Background Audio”. A locked screen or sleeping computer never counts.

What's the difference between blocking and limiting?

A blocked site is always closed. A limited site is open until you've used its daily budget. Limited sites are also the ones your schedule blocks, so your limits list doubles as your list of distractions.

Can I unblock a site if I really need it?

Yes, from the extension popup at any time. If you've turned on Strict Mode you'll first need to type a short sentence and wait 30 seconds. That delay is deliberate.

Can Strict Mode be bypassed?

Honestly, yes: no extension can stop you from turning it off in Chrome's settings. Strict Mode is designed to add enough friction to stop impulsive decisions, not to lock you out of your own browser.

Does it work in Incognito windows?

Only if you allow it. Chrome keeps extensions out of Incognito by default; you can enable it under Manage extension → Allow in Incognito. Once allowed, your blocks, limits and schedule apply there too, and Incognito time counts toward the same daily limits. Keep in mind that sites visited in Incognito are then saved in your stats and history like any others.

Will it slow down my browser?

No. It checks in every 30 seconds and saves in small batches. It doesn't add any scripts to the pages you visit and never reads what's on them.

Which browsers are supported?

Time & Limit is built for Google Chrome. Other Chromium-based browsers may work, but they aren't officially tested.

Install manually

  1. Download or clone the extension's source code.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extension folder.
  4. Pin Time & Limit from the puzzle-piece menu in the toolbar.
