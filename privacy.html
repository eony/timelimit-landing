Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 09, 2026

Introduction

"Time & Limit" (the "Extension") is developed by Eon. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is stored.

Data Collection and Usage

What We Collect

To provide the core functionality of tracking your web usage and limiting distractions, the Extension collects the following data locally on your device:

  • Browsing Activity: The URLs of the websites you visit.
  • Time Data: The duration of time spent on active tabs and audible background tabs.

How We Use Data

This data is used strictly locally within the Extension to:

  • Display your daily usage statistics in the dashboard.
  • Enforce the time limits you set for specific websites.
  • Block access to websites you have explicitly listed as "Blocked".

Data Storage

All data collected by "Time & Limit" is stored locally on your computer using the Chrome Storage API.

  • No Data Transfer: We do not transmit your browsing history, time data, or settings to any external servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics services.
  • No Third-Party Access: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.

Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • tabs: To detect the currently active website and track time.
  • storage: To save your settings, limits, and daily usage stats locally.
  • alarms: To perform periodic checks for time limits.
  • notifications: To alert you when a time limit is reached.
  • scripting: To ensure blocking functionality works correctly.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): Required to track time on any website you visit.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official web store or support channels.
