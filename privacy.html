Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Introduction

"Time & Limit" (the "Extension") is developed by Eon. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how it is stored.

Data Collection and Usage

What We Collect

To provide the core functionality of tracking your web usage and limiting distractions, the Extension collects the following data locally on your device:

  • Browsing Activity: The addresses (URLs) of the pages open in your tabs. They are used to tell which website you are on and to apply your rules. Only the website's domain (for example, youtube.com) is saved with your usage; full addresses are not kept in your history.
  • Time Data: The time spent on the active tab, and on tabs playing audio or video (background tabs only if you turn on "Track Background Audio"), including a breakdown by hour of day.

Incognito Windows

The Extension does not run in Incognito windows unless you turn on "Allow in Incognito" in Chrome's extension settings. If you do:

  • Your blocks, limits and schedule apply in Incognito windows as well.
  • Time spent on websites in Incognito windows is tracked and saved on your device together with the rest of your usage. Those websites will appear in your statistics, history, exports and backups, and they remain there after the Incognito window is closed.
  • Incognito time counts toward the same daily limits as regular browsing.

If you do not want Incognito browsing to be recorded, leave "Allow in Incognito" turned off.

How We Use Data

This data is used strictly locally within the Extension to:

  • Display your daily usage statistics in the dashboard.
  • Enforce the time limits you set for specific websites.
  • Block access to websites you have explicitly listed as "Blocked", addresses containing words you have blocked, and limited websites during the hours you have scheduled.
  • Show notifications and the toolbar badge that tell you how much time is left on a limited website.

Data Storage

All data collected by "Time & Limit" is stored locally on your computer using the Chrome Storage API.

  • No Data Transfer: We do not transmit your browsing history, time data, or settings to any external servers, cloud databases, or third-party analytics services. Fonts and site icons are loaded locally, so the Extension's pages make no external requests.
  • Optional Settings Sync: If you turn on "Sync Settings", your limits, block lists and preferences (not your browsing or usage data) are stored with Chrome Sync so they appear on your other signed-in Chrome devices. This is handled by Google Chrome, not by us, and is off by default.
  • Exports: Backups and CSV exports are only created when you ask for them and are saved as files on your computer.
  • No Third-Party Access: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.

Retention and Deletion

  • Daily history is kept for up to 400 days; older days are deleted automatically.
  • "Reset Today" in the settings clears the time tracked today.
  • Removing the Extension from Chrome deletes all data it stored on your device. Settings saved with Chrome Sync are managed through your Google account.

Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions to function:

  • tabs: To detect the currently active website and track time.
  • storage: To save your settings, limits, and daily usage stats locally.
  • alarms: To perform periodic checks for time limits.
  • notifications: To warn you before and when a time limit is reached.
  • idle: To pause time tracking while you are away from the computer.
  • favicon: To show website icons from Chrome's local icon cache.
  • The Extension does not request access to the content of websites and adds no scripts to the pages you visit. It only sees each tab's address (via tabs) to track time and apply your rules.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
